03 · How a project runs

Managed like a project, not a favour.

Ten years of running engineering and IT programmes means you always know what is done, what is next, and what it costs.

15-minute call

You click through your own system with your own fields and sample data before the main build starts.

Step 3 · Weeks 2+

Weekly demos, a shared progress tracker, and sign-off at the end of each phase. No surprises on the invoice.

Go live & support

Data import, staff training in Arabic or English, then hosting, backups and support on a monthly plan.

05 · Questions

Common questions

Do I own the system and the data?

Yes. Your data is always yours and can be exported at any time. On custom builds you also receive the full source code.

Can it work in Arabic?

Every system supports Arabic and English, including right-to-left layouts, printed invoices and receipts.

Where is it hosted?

On secure cloud hosting with daily backups, under your own domain. If you prefer, I can install it on your company's own server.

What if I only need a small change to an existing system?

Send me the details on WhatsApp or email. Small jobs are quoted at a fixed price before any work starts.
