06 · Questions

Common questions

Do I own the system and the data?

Yes. Your data is always yours and can be exported at any time. On custom builds you also receive the full source code.

Which languages and currencies are supported?

English as standard. Sinhala, Tamil and other languages, including right-to-left scripts, can be added. Systems work in your currency and can handle tax rules for your country.

Will you sign an NDA and a contract?

Yes. Every project starts with a written scope, a fixed quote and an agreement. I am happy to sign your NDA before we discuss details.

Where is it hosted?

On secure cloud hosting with daily backups, under your own domain. If you prefer, I can install it on your company's own server.

Can you work on a system someone else built?

Yes. I start with a short review of the code and give you a fixed quote for the fixes or new features before any work begins.
